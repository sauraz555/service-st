# Interlace Studies - Statement of Services Generator

Official single-page Statement of Services generator and financial ledger for **Interlace Studies Pty Ltd** (Migration & Education Consultants).

## Features
- **Interlace Brand Typography**: Styled with `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter`, matching the official wordmark and aesthetic.
- **Clear Type Scale**: 12px uppercase section labels, 14px body and inputs, 20-24px headers.
- **Card-Structured Form**: Distinct card containers with brand-blue left-border accents separating Client/Matter and Financial details.
- **GST-Inclusive Direct Pricing**: Fixed fee entered directly as the total inclusive of GST; automatically calculates Ex-GST and GST components.
- **Driver Field Highlight**: Subtle background tint and border highlighting the Fixed Service Fee as the primary calculation driver.
- **Zero-Friction Inputs**: No pesky default `0.00` text to backspace—clean placeholders and auto-select on focus.
- **Universal Application Support**: Phrasing tailored universally for both Visa and Skills Assessment applications.
- **High-Fidelity MARA Statement Print**: Pixel-perfect A4 print/PDF layout with Interlace letterhead, financial position card, and compliance declarations.
